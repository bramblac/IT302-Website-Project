ROUTE GUIDE

Plan Your Next Run

Choosing a route that matches your fitness level can make running more enjoyable. Use these distance ideas to plan your next outing around Harrisonburg.

Distances below are general training suggestions, not verified measurements of specific local trails.

Short Run

Suggested distance: 1–3 miles

Short runs are useful for beginners, recovery days, or anyone looking for a quick workout.

Planning tip: Choose a familiar route and keep a comfortable pace.

Medium Run

Suggested distance: 3–6 miles

Medium runs can help regular runners build endurance while enjoying more time outside.

Planning tip: Bring water when needed and consider the hills and surface along the route.

Long Run

Suggested distance: 6 miles or more

Long runs help experienced runners spend more time on their feet and gradually develop endurance.

Planning tip: Plan water stops, check the weather, and increase your distance gradually.

Try a Run-Walk Workout

Alternating running and walking can be a helpful way to build consistency or return to running.

Example Workout

  • Run for 4 minutes at a comfortable pace.
  • Walk for 1 minute to recover.
  • Repeat the intervals as needed.

Adjust the intervals to your fitness level, and stop if you feel unwell or experience concerning pain.

Before You Leave

  • Check the weather and daylight hours.
  • Review the route and its total distance.
  • Check trail access, conditions, and parking.
  • Bring a charged phone and water when appropriate.
  • Choose a route that matches your experience.
