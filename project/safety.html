SAFETY TIPS

Stay Safe on the Trail

A little preparation can help make every run safer and more enjoyable. Follow these tips before heading out around Harrisonburg and the Shenandoah Valley.

Bring Water

Carry water on longer runs, especially during warm weather. Drink regularly and pay attention to how you feel throughout your run.

Check the Weather

Check the forecast before leaving. Dress for the temperature, prepare for changing conditions, and avoid running during dangerous weather.

Wear Visible Clothing

Wear bright or reflective clothing when running in low light. Use a light when running in the dark, and remain aware of nearby traffic.

Respect Other Trail Users

Be aware of cyclists, walkers, and other runners. Follow posted signs, stay on marked paths, and give other trail users enough space to pass safely.

Plan Your Run

  • Tell someone where you are going and when you expect to return.
  • Carry a charged phone for emergencies.
  • Choose a route that matches your experience and fitness level.
  • Check trail access, conditions, and daylight hours before leaving.
  • Consider running with a partner on unfamiliar trails.
